JalRaksha



The Flood Relief, Reminder \& Safety Expert



1\. Overview



JalRaksha is an AI-powered flood safety, relief and emergency-response platform designed to provide people with localized, understandable and actionable information before and during flood situations.



The platform does not aim to replace official disaster-warning systems. Instead, it acts as a last-mile safety and intelligence layer that takes trusted environmental, geographic and official information and converts it into personalized flood-risk information, impact assessment, alerts, safe-zone recommendations, safest-route navigation and emergency communication.



The core idea is:



Trusted Data → Flood Risk → Local Impact → Explanation → Alert → Safe Zone → Safest Route → Action → Community Feedback



\---



2\. AI-Powered Flood Risk Assessment



JalRaksha collects and processes relevant information such as:



\- Rainfall

\- River/water levels

\- Soil moisture

\- Temperature

\- Elevation

\- Distance from rivers

\- Historical flood information

\- Geographic information

\- Population and infrastructure information

\- Citizen reports



The AI engine analyzes these inputs to estimate the potential flood risk of a particular village or location.



Instead of simply telling the user:



«"There may be a flood."»



JalRaksha aims to tell the user:



«"Your area is at high risk, these locations may be affected, this is why the risk is high, and this is what you should do."»



The system can generate:



\- Flood Risk Score

\- Population Exposure

\- House Risk

\- Road Risk

\- Farm Risk

\- School Risk

\- Hospital Risk

\- Infrastructure Risk

\- Confidence Level

\- Data Freshness



AI-generated assessments must be clearly distinguished from official government warnings.

3\. Village-Level Flood Impact Assessment



When flood risk is detected, JalRaksha analyzes the surrounding geographic area to determine what could potentially be affected.



For example:



Village X — HIGH RISK



\- 320 houses potentially affected

\- 185 acres of farmland potentially affected

\- 1 school potentially affected

\- Road X at high risk

\- Livestock potentially affected

\- Nearby infrastructure potentially affected



This gives people and administrators more useful information than a generic flood warning.

4\. "Why Am I Getting This Warning?"



Users should be able to tap a "Why?" button on an alert.



JalRaksha can explain the factors contributing to the risk, such as:



\- Heavy rainfall forecast

\- Increasing river level

\- High soil saturation

\- Low-lying geographical location

\- High water levels near the area

\- Similar conditions during previous flood events



The application can display:



Flood Risk: 84%

Confidence: High

Data Updated: 7 minutes ago



This makes the AI system more transparent and helps users understand why they are receiving a warning.



\---



5\. Flood Alert Levels



JalRaksha uses different alert levels instead of treating every situation as an emergency.



🟢 LOW — Normal



No immediate action required.



🟡 WATCH — Monitor



The user should monitor the situation and continue checking updates.



🟠 PREPARE — Get Ready



Users should prepare emergency supplies, medicines, important documents, livestock and other essential items.



🔴 CRITICAL — Immediate Attention



Users should follow official evacuation instructions and move toward an appropriate safe area when instructed.



This approach helps reduce unnecessary alerts and alert fatigue.

6\. Three-Channel Notification System



JalRaksha provides three major notification channels.



A. App Notification



Users with the JalRaksha application receive real-time personalized notifications.



Example:



«🔴 HIGH FLOOD RISK

Flood impact may occur near your location.

Nearest safe area: Community Hall

Safest available route identified.»



\---



B. SMS Notification



Users can receive important flood alerts through SMS even when they are not actively using the application.



This is particularly useful when:



\- The app is closed

\- Internet connectivity is poor

\- The user does not have mobile data

\- The user needs a simple emergency message



\---



C. Emergency Contact SMS



A major feature of JalRaksha is that users can add trusted contacts who do not need to install the application.



For example:



Emergency Circle



\- Father

\- Mother

\- Brother

\- Relative

\- Trusted Friend



When a critical flood situation occurs, JalRaksha can send an SMS to selected emergency contacts.



Example:



«JalRaksha Critical Alert:

Your contact \[Name] may be in a high flood-risk area near \[Location]. Please contact them and follow official emergency instructions.»



This creates a safety network around the user even when family members or friends don't use the application.

7\. Emergency Circle



Users can add trusted people to their Emergency Circle.



Each contact can contain:



\- Name

\- Phone number

\- Relationship

\- Notification preference

\- Verification status



Users can decide which alerts their contacts should receive.



For example:



Prepare Alerts: OFF

Critical Alerts: ON



This prevents emergency contacts from receiving unnecessary notifications.

JalRaksha can provide two quick emergency actions:

🆘 Need Help

9\. Safe Zone Detection



When the user's location is at high flood risk, JalRaksha identifies nearby designated safe areas.



These can include:



\- Official shelters

\- Relief centres

\- Community halls

\- Designated evacuation centres

\- Other verified safe locations



The application can show:



🟢 Nearest Safe Zone



Community Hall

Distance: 2.4 km

Estimated travel time: 8 minutes



The system should prioritize officially designated or verified safe locations.

10\. Safest Available Route



One of the major features of JalRaksha is Safe Route Navigation.



The application does not simply find the shortest route.



It attempts to find the safest available route based on current information.



The routing system can consider:



\- Flood-risk zones

\- Road closures

\- Water levels

\- Road elevation

\- River proximity

\- Bridges

\- Citizen flood reports

\- Current road conditions

\- Officially restricted roads

\- Distance



For example:



Route A



Distance: 1.8 km

Flood Risk: HIGH

Road Status: Unsafe



❌ Not Recommended



Route B



Distance: 2.4 km

Flood Risk: LOW

Road Status: Open



🟢 Safest Available Route



The application can display:



Your Location → Road B → Main Road → Safe Zone



and provide a:



VIEW SAFEST ROUTE



button.



JalRaksha should never claim that a route is "100% safe." It should be presented as the safest available/recommended route based on currently available data.



\---



11\. Dynamic Safe Route Recalculation



Flood conditions can change quickly.



Therefore, the route should be dynamic.



For example:



User starts travelling

↓

Flooding is reported on Road A

↓

Road A risk increases

↓

Current route becomes unsafe

↓

JalRaksha recalculates

↓

New safer route is displayed



The user receives:



«⚠️ Route Updated

Flooding has been reported on your current route.

A safer alternative route has been identified.»



This makes JalRaksha different from a normal navigation application.

22\. The Main Innovation



JalRaksha is not simply another flood-alert application.



The main innovation is the connection between:



Flood Risk → Local Impact → Explanation → Safe Location → Safest Route → Action → Emergency Communication



Existing systems can provide valuable flood information and official warnings. JalRaksha focuses on the last-mile problem:



«"A person has received a flood warning. What does that warning actually mean for them, where should they go, which route should they take, and who should know that they may be in danger?"»



JalRaksha attempts to answer all of these questions through a single platform.



Final Vision



JalRaksha — The Flood Relief, Reminder \& Safety Expert



Know the Risk. Understand the Impact. Find Safety. Take Action.



JalRaksha aims to transform a conventional flood warning into a complete safety journey:



Warning → Understanding → Safe Zone → Safest Route → Action → Relief → Recovery



The platform is designed to help individuals, families, communities and administrators respond more intelligently to flood situations while clearly respecting and complementing official disaster-management systems.
